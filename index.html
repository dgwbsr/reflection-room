How the points are found

  1. Mirror the source. Reflect the speaker across the wall, outside the room.
  2. Draw to the listener. A straight line from the mirrored source to the listener crosses the wall.
  3. Mark the crossing. That point is the first reflection point. The incoming and outgoing angles match.

Delays use 343 m/s, the speed of sound at about 20 °C. This plan covers the four walls; ceiling and floor points follow the same mirror method.
